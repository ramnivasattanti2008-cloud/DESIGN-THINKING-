10 Standout Technologies

Why MIRROR is intellectually different for the iQOO Hackathon:
🥇 1. 🧠 Intention → Physical State Engine
Don't say "Turn off the AC." Say "I'm leaving." MIRROR understands what the physical world should become.
🥈 2. 🔮 Physical Consequence Engine
Reasons about consequences, not objects: "Leaving + Window open = rain damage & security hazard."
🥉 3. 🌎 Temporary World Model
Structured tree representation of reality (Room → AC 24°C, Window OPEN, TV OFF) rather than isolated image labels.
4. 👁️ Active Perception
Understands uncertainty: "I can't determine what this device is. Point camera at the switch panel."
5. 🔄 Closed-Loop Reality Verification
Plan → Action → Observe Again → Compare → Verify. Proves physical state actually changed with camera sensors.
6. ⚠️ Consequence + Risk + Reversibility
3-tier safety gate: Low risk executes; Medium risk asks; High consequence refuses or requires physical confirmation.
7. 🧩 Dependency Reasoning
Decomposes prerequisite chains (e.g. Presentation → Projector → HDMI connected). Pinpoints exact blockers.
8. 🕐 State Memory — Not Surveillance
Physical delta tracking from explicit snapshots (08:30 vs 13:00 vs 18:00) without permanent video recording.
9. 🗣️ Human Correction → Learning
Explicit controllable preferences: "You usually keep the AC on for short departures. Keep it on?"
10. 🎯 "What Should Happen?" Philosophy
Traditional CV asks: "What is there?" MIRROR asks: "Given user intention, what should the physical environment look like?"
